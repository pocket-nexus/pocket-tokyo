# Repository instructions

- Pocket Tokyo owns the export page (`web/src/pocket`, `web/export.html`), the city compiler and the pack (`crates/tokyo-cook`, `crates/tokyo-pack`), what moves and what a frame draws (`crates/tokyo-sim`), the interface (`ui/`, `crates/tokyo-interface`), the device profiles and area files (`profiles/`, `areas/`) and the runtimes (`vita/`, `psp/`, `n3ds/`). The rest of `web/` is the model: Procedural Tokyo by Yong Su, under its own MIT notice (`web/LICENSE`); keep that notice. PocketJS (pinned in `vendor/pocketjs`) owns the device toolchains, the Vita dev host and wired debug transport, VPK packaging and the GXM device kernel.
- Do not edit the submodule to fix application behavior. Send reusable changes to PocketJS, then update the pinned revision here.
- **This repository stays private** until its owner says otherwise. Do not publish the repository, a build, a pack or captures to a public place. A compiled area is a derived database of its sources (PLATEAU, OpenStreetMap under the ODbL, GSI): raw downloads, tiles, CityIR and packs stay out of Git, under the ignored `web/data`, `web/public/tiles` and `.pocket-build/`.
- Use Conventional Commits for commits and pull requests (`type(scope): summary`). Publish validated changes as a Draft PR.
- The look of a surface is defined once, by the reference's mesher and materials. The export page (`web/src/pocket/export.js`) runs them and records what they produce; the compiler lowers that. Do not re-implement a material in the compiler or in a device shader: change the reference, or how the export draws it, and export again.
- A change to the export, the compiler or a profile goes through `bun tools/tokyo.ts export` and `cook`; the pack and its receipt are outputs under `.pocket-build/`. A device difference is a profile value or a lowering in `crates/tokyo-cook`, not a branch in the export.
- The pack's tables and vertex layouts are `crates/tokyo-pack`; the compiler and every runtime read them from there. A layout change raises `VERSION`.
- `tokyo-sim` and `tokyo-pack` build without `std` (`default-features = false`): `core`, `alloc` and `libm` only. What moves (camera, tour, clock, shadows, traffic) lives in `tokyo-sim` with a host test, and a device only draws it.
- The three scene programs take their per-frame values as `uFrame` and `uLook` (`vita/shaders/frame.cg`). Light and tint reach the fragments through `COLOR0` and `COLOR1`; a texture lookup's coordinates are the `.xy` of a `TEXCOORD` of their own. A lookup at `.zw`, or computed in the fragment program, costs a millisecond or more over the frame.
- The frame budget is 16.6 ms on the Vita at 960 × 544 with 4× multisampling. The scene costs 3.0 ms plus 0.053 ms per 1 000 triangles; a draw call costs 2 µs. A renderer change reports `bun tools/tokyo.ts bench` before and after.
- GXM keeps a scene's transformed vertices in its parameter buffer; when the scene fills it, the frame takes three times as long. `vita/build.rs` and `__wrap_sceGxmInitialize` set its size (vita2d does not expose it). A program that adds interpolants fills it sooner.
- To time the GPU, send `{"profile": true}`: the scene then goes to an off-screen target with its own completion notice (`sceneMs`). The display scene's own notice can sit at the refresh and reads about 15 ms whatever is drawn.
- Shader programs are compiled on the device by SceShaccCg and cached by source hash under `ux0:data/pocket-tokyo/gxp`. Mixing `half` and `float` in `lerp` is an overload ambiguity there: cast explicitly.
- The pack is copied from the USB share to `ux0:data/pocket-tokyo/city.pack` when `city.json` beside it differs from the one on the card, and read from the card. `tools/vita.ts sync` writes both.
- The Pocket3D title card plays first at every launch (`pocket3d_title::vita::play()` in `vita/src/main.rs`). It comes from PocketJS: do not skip, shorten, recolour or redraw it here. A development run skips it with `{"title": false}` in the share's `tokyo/boot.json`.
- The app icon on every console is the Pocket3D icon from `vendor/pocketjs/engine/pocket3d/icon/`: `psp/Psp.toml` names `psp/ICON0.PNG`, `tools/vita.ts` passes `POCKET3D_ICON.vita` to `packageVitaVpk`, `n3ds/Makefile` gives `smdhtool` `3ds/icon.png` and `3ds/icon-small.png`. **This repository holds no icon file**: do not add, draw, capture or resize one, and a new console target takes its icon from the same directory. The cover and LiveArea pictures (`psp/assets/pic1.png`, `vita/assets/sce_sys/livearea/contents/`) are captures of this game and stay here. The procedure is `vendor/pocketjs/skills/pocket3d-brand/SKILL.md`; `tools/icon.test.ts` (in `bun run test`) fails when an icon file is tracked or a build file stops naming the Pocket3D one.
- The interface is one PocketJS app, `ui/`, drawn over the city on every device. **A device crate draws no text and no 2D**: instruments, lists, the map and touch controls go in `ui/`. A device differs by its presentation (`ui/app/presentations/`, chosen by modality in `ui/pocket.json`), not by a fork of a shared part: what the clock, a row or the map looks like is in `ui/app/parts.tsx` once. The procedure is `vendor/pocketjs/skills/pocket3d-interface/SKILL.md`.
- `ui/app/protocol.ts` and `crates/tokyo-interface` are the two sides of one protocol. Change both in one commit, with `cargo test -p tokyo-interface` and `bun tools/ui.ts test`. The flow (the title, the flight, the menu) is `tokyo_interface::Session` around `tokyo_sim::flight::Flight`; a device does not keep its own, and a device's pad reaches the flight through `tokyo_interface::Pad`.
- A number that changes during a flight is a member of `t` written to its node through `@pocketjs/framework/hot`, never a signal. A timer in `ui/` goes through `createPulse` (it counts what is pending, and `idle` rests on that count), and a screen is wrapped in `Keep` instead of being mounted on a mode change. A device takes a turn of the guest when `tokyo_interface::Pace` says it is due.
- A named place is a row of the area file (`areas/<area>.json` `places`: name, latitude, longitude, optional `reach` in metres). The interface compiles them through `tools/ui.ts`; do not write a place name in `ui/`.
- Look at every screen with `bun tools/ui.ts preview` before a device build. A change to the interface reports the device's `bench` like a renderer change (the guest's turn and its draw are in the status record).
- Keep captures, logs, CityIR, packs, USB-share contents and build receipts in `.pocket-build/`. Put reproducible commands, results and limits in the PR description instead of committing them.
- Separate evidence kinds when reporting: host build and tests, compile receipt, reference capture, device frame timing (`bench`), device capture, and what a person saw on the console.
- A landmark's look is its members. A model that builds a landmark (`web/src/world/tower.js`) reports beams, boxes and lamps through `web/src/world/landmark.js`; the export writes them to `landmarks.cir`; `crates/tokyo-cook/src/landmark.rs` makes each level of detail from the members. Do not model a landmark in the compiler or on a device, and do not stand a picture on a few faces in for one.
- A handheld pack keeps each cell's near level as one record of `NEAR`. A record must fit a device slot: the compiler coarsens a cell's painted geometry until it does (`near.room` in the profile). The 3DS pack rides in the `.3dsx`, which the wire installs up to 32 MiB.
- PSP: the frame budget is 33.3 ms at 30 frames a second. A list costs 1.5 ms plus 0.63 ms per 1 000 triangles whatever its state; a renderer change reports `bun tools/psp.ts bench` before and after. Ground pictures use indices below 128: the upper half of a palette is the device's, for shadows. Only the frame thread allocates; the reading thread and the shadow thread write into memory they were given.
- PSP: `bun tools/psp.ts run` resets PSPLINK and uses the share of whichever `usbhostfs_pc` is running. It takes PocketJS's `psp:usb` lease; do not pass `--take` over another session's run.
- 3DS: citro3d reads the texture it is handed to `C3D_TexBind`: never pass `NULL` for units 1 and 2. A texture unit that stays bound is read at the vertex program's texture coordinates, so every program writes all three. Close a file on the ROMFS before `romfsExit`: the C library closes what is left open after the ROMFS is gone, and the program faults on its way out, which is also the way to the next installed build.
- 3DS: installs are `.3dsx` only, over the paired wire (`bun tools/n3ds.ts install`); never build or suggest a CIA. The pairing key is a local file under `vendor/pocketjs/.pocket/`, ignored by Git.
- `bun tools/psp.ts emu` (PPSSPPHeadless) and `bun tools/n3ds.ts emu` (Azahar, software renderer) run the same binaries as the consoles and write a frame and a status. They show what is drawn, not how long it takes: Azahar forgives a read of unmapped memory that stops the console, so read its log for `unmapped`.
